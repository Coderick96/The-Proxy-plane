# Portal monogram

Asset: public/proxy-plane-icon.png
Created with the built-in image-generation tool for The Proxy Plane.

Design prompt: A finished square app icon with two bold black uppercase P
letters, stacked and staggered (upper left / lower right), over a luminous
blue-green magical portal. Cyan, teal and emerald swirls with a bright center,
crisp open counters and a clean dominant monogram. Rounded square, front-facing,
no additional words, cards or unrelated symbols.

Revision 2: public/proxy-plane-icon-v2.png. Built-in image edit: preserve the
blue-green portal; make the black P letters smaller and more vertically stacked
with a slight horizontal stagger, then add the exact black uppercase wording
PROXY PLANE beneath the monogram. Used in the header, favicon and touch icon.

Revision 3: public/proxy-plane-icon-v3.png. Built-in image edit: preserve the
portal, smaller stacked black P letters and exact PROXY PLANE text. Surround
them with five colored mana-symbol medallions: white sun at top, blue droplet
upper right, black skull lower right, red flame lower left, green tree upper
left. Keep every symbol separated from the monogram and wording.

Revision 4: public/proxy-plane-icon-v4.png. Built-in image edit: change only
the PROXY PLANE wordmark coloring to ivory-white, blue, charcoal-black, red
and green to match the surrounding mana symbols. Keep the stacked P letters
black and preserve the portal, medallions, typography and layout.
